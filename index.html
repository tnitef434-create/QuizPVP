Tutorial - War Card Game

🎴 How to Play

War is a classic card battle game. Each round, you and your opponent flip a card. The highest card wins the round and earns 1 point!

The game lasts 7 rounds. Whoever has the most points at the end wins the match.

Card Values (Lowest to Highest)

2
2 = Lowest
3-10
Number Cards
J
Jack = 11
Q
Queen = 12
K
King = 13
A
Ace = 14 (Highest!)

♠ Card Suits

♠ Spades
♥ Hearts
♦ Diamonds
♣ Clubs

Note: Suits don't affect card strength. Only the card value matters!

WAR Rounds!

When both players flip the same card value, it's WAR!

War rounds are worth 2 points instead of 1. The winner is determined randomly, making wars exciting and unpredictable!

Example: You flip a 7♠, opponent flips a 7♥ → WAR!

Game Modes

  • 1v1 Multiplayer: Battle real players online. Win to earn points and XP!
  • VS Bot: Practice against AI. No rewards, just for fun and learning!

Rewards (1v1 Only)

Victory
+10 Points, +20 XP
Draw
+3 Points, +5 XP
Defeat
+1 Point, +2 XP

Bot games give 0 rewards - they're just for practice!

Pro Tips

  • Cards are randomly shuffled each game - it's all about luck!
  • Hope for WAR rounds - they're worth double points!
  • Practice with bots to learn before facing real players
  • Even if you lose, you still earn points for playing!
